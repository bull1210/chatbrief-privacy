How it works

Two ways to add a group. Zero hassle.

Import a past chat export, or let ChatBrief auto-detect your active groups from notifications.

📂 Method 1 — Manual Import

Open WhatsApp

Go to any group chat → tap the three dots → More → Export Chat.

Share to ChatBrief

Choose "Without Media" then select ChatBrief from the share sheet. The file opens instantly.

Name your group

Give the group a name and colour. ChatBrief imports all messages automatically.

Tap Summarize

Select a date range, tap Summarize, and get a structured digest in seconds.

🔔 Method 2 — Auto-Detect via Notifications

Grant access once

Enable Notification Access for ChatBrief in Settings → Special App Access.

ChatBrief watches quietly

In the background, ChatBrief spots active WhatsApp groups from your incoming notifications.

Get notified

When a new group is detected, you get a push notification: "New group found: Family Group".

Add in one tap

Open the detected groups list, select the ones you want, tap Add — messages already captured.
