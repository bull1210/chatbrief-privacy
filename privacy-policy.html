Privacy Policy

App: ChatBrief  |  Package: com.chatbrief.app  |  Last updated: 6 Jul 2026

ChatBrief ("we", "our", or "the app") is a WhatsApp chat summarization tool that helps you digest group and personal conversations quickly. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Data We Access

  • WhatsApp notifications (live groups): With your permission (Notification Access), the app reads incoming WhatsApp notifications for groups you choose to add, to detect new messages as they arrive. Groups you haven't added are only staged locally so you can review and add them — nothing is summarized until you do.
  • WhatsApp chat exports (manual import): You can also manually export chats from WhatsApp and share them with ChatBrief. The app reads the text content of these exports solely to generate a summary.
  • In both cases, message text is stored locally on your device in an encrypted (SQLCipher) database and is never stored permanently on our servers.
  • API keys (optional): If you choose "Bring Your Own Key" mode with your own Google AI Studio key, that key is stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted DataStore. It is never transmitted to our servers.
  • App preferences and summaries: Settings (such as selected groups, filters, and summarization history) are stored locally on your device using Room database and DataStore. This data does not leave your device.

2. Data Sent to Third-Party AI Services

When a summary is generated, the relevant chat messages are sent to:

  • Google Gemini API (Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite) – operated by Google LLC, either through ChatBrief's own server-side key pool (default) or your own key if you've enabled BYOK mode. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms.

Free-trial and standard server-plan usage may be served using Google's free-tier API, for which Google's terms permit use of prompt/response data to improve their products. Paid subscription plans and BYOK with your own paid-tier key are served through Google's paid tier, which Google does not use to train its models. We do not control how Google processes data once received, and recommend reviewing its policies directly. Message content is sent only at the moment of summarization and is not stored by us after the request completes.

Because group chats include messages from people other than you, please only add groups where you're a legitimate participant and be mindful of the expectations of other members — they have not individually consented to AI processing of their messages.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use the following Firebase services (operated by Google LLC) to improve app stability and understand usage patterns:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Automatically collects crash reports including device model, OS version, and stack traces. No personally identifiable information or chat content is included in crash reports.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymized usage events (e.g., screens viewed, features used). No chat content is included.

These services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Backend Infrastructure

ChatBrief uses Supabase for backend services (device/subscription management and routing AI summarization requests through our server-side key pool). Supabase is subject to its own Privacy Policy. We do not store your chat content on Supabase beyond the brief moment a summarization request is in transit — server logs record only message counts, device identifiers, and error codes, never message text.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

If you choose to subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Razorpay. We store your plan type and subscription status; we do not store your card or payment instrument details — those are handled entirely by Razorpay, subject to its own Privacy Policy.

6. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your WhatsApp account credentials.
  • We do not store your chat messages on our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use paid-tier or BYOK requests to train AI models. Free-tier requests may be used by Google to improve its models, per Google's own terms — see Section 2.

7. Data Retention

Messages, summaries, and preferences are stored locally on your device and remain under your control. You can clear all app data at any time via Settings → Delete All Data, or via Android Settings → Apps → ChatBrief → Clear Data. We do not retain your chat content on our servers beyond the duration of a single summarization request.

8. Children's Privacy

ChatBrief is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Security

API keys are stored using Android's encrypted DataStore. Messages and summaries are stored in a SQLCipher-encrypted local database. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your data, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
